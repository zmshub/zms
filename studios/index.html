FAQ

Good to know.

Still unsure? Email us and we'll answer within 24 hours.

Do you offer marketing, SEO or ads management?

No. ZMS is a creative, Generative and tech studio: we make the visuals, brands, websites and Generative systems. We don't run social media accounts, SEO or ad campaigns.

Do you edit videos?

We don't edit your existing footage. Our Generative Videos, Reels & Shorts are entirely new, generated footage.

How fast is delivery?

Most Generative image, headshot and logo packages arrive in 24–72 hours. Websites take 5–21 days and apps 1–4 weeks. The clock starts once payment and your brief are in.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work remotely with brands, agencies and creators worldwide. Every package is priced in USD, with INR shown alongside.

How do I pay?

Send your brief and we reply with a quote and a secure payment link. Fixed packages are paid upfront; custom projects of ₹10,000 / $150 or more are 50% to start and 50% before handoff. We don't do refund & return ✌️

Who owns the work?

Once paid, you can use the final deliverables for your business. Generated images can't always be registered as trademarks, so for a trademark we recommend our Pro or Premium logo with vector files and your own legal check.

What extra costs could there be?

Only third-party fees you choose: domain and hosting, Generative/API usage for chatbots, or automation tool subscriptions. We tell you upfront.

Can I get something not listed?

Yes. Tell us what you need and we will quote it within 24 hours.
