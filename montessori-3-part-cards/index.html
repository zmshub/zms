Questions

What are 3-part cards?

A classic Montessori material. Each word has a control card (picture and word together), a picture card and a label card. The child matches pictures to the control card, then labels to pictures.

What is inside?

5 themed sets: Animals (8), Shapes (9), Everyday Things (9), Weather (7) and Feelings (6). That is 39 words and 117 cards, plus a how-to-use guide.

What ages is it for?

Ages 2.5 to 6. Younger children match pictures; older children read the labels.

What do I need?

A printer, thick white card (200 to 250 gsm), scissors and optionally a laminator.

What paper sizes are included?
