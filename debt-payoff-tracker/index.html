Questions

What is the difference between Snowball and Avalanche?

Snowball pays the smallest balance first for quick wins and motivation. Avalanche pays the highest interest rate first, which usually costs the least interest. The tracker runs both on your debts so you can compare the debt-free date and total interest.

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. Upload the .xlsx file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets (File, Import, Upload). It also works in Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice and Numbers.

Which currency does it use?

Any. The sheet only works with numbers, so type your balances in dollars, pounds, euros, rupees or any currency you like.

How many debts can I enter?

No. It is a planning tool. Interest is estimated monthly with fixed rates, so your lender's figures may differ slightly.
