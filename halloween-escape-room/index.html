Questions

What ages is it for?

Ages 8 to 13 play it on their own. Children from about 6 can join with a grown-up reading aloud and helping.

How long does it take?

Most teams should plan for 45 to 60 minutes. The host guide has a timeline and a way to make it shorter or longer.

Do I need padlocks, apps or internet?

No. Every puzzle page has a Door Lock: players turn their answer into numbers (A=1, B=2…) and add them up. If the total matches the page, the next envelope opens. You only need a printer, scissors, six envelopes and pencils.

What do I get after paying?

A download folder with the Host Guide and Answer Key, the Player Pack in colour and the Player Pack in ink-saver black and white, each in both A4 and US Letter. The link is shown right after payment and emailed to you.

Is it scary?

It is spooky fun: friendly ghosts, bats, a cauldron and a witch with a happy ending. No gore, no violence and nothing gruesome.

How many players?

One to eight in a team. For a party with more guests, print one set per team and race.

Can I use it in a classroom or at a party?

Yes. Print as many copies as you need for your own home, party or classroom. Please do not resell or share the files.
