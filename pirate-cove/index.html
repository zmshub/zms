Ages 8 to 14, 1 to 8 players. Younger kids can play with a grown-up reading along, and the host guide has ways to make it easier or harder.

No. Every page has a Treasure Lock: players turn their answer into numbers and add them up, and the right total tells them they can open the next envelope. Just print, cut and play.

How long does it take?

Plan 45 to 60 minutes. The host guide has a timeline and a 10-minute setup.
