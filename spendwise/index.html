FAQ

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. You get an Excel .xlsx file and a Google Sheets version: open the link, click "Make a copy" and it is yours. Charts and formulas work in both.

Is this a budget planner?

It is a money tracker: you log what actually came in and went out, and it shows where your salary went, your UPI spends, savings rate and 50/30/20 split automatically.

Which months does it cover?

All 12 months of the Indian financial year, April to March, with 300 spend rows per month.

Can I change the categories?

Yes. Rename any category on the Setup tab, add your own in the spare rows and choose whether it counts as a Need, Want or Saving.
