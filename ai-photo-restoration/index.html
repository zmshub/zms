FAQ

What photos can you restore?

Old prints with scratches, cracks, creases, tears, stains, mould spots or fading, plus blurry or low-contrast black & white photos. A scan is best, but a sharp phone photo of the print taken flat in daylight works too.

What do I get?

For each photo (up to 2 per order): a cleaned, repaired black & white version and a naturally colourized version, as high-resolution JPGs, by email within 24 hours.

Will faces stay the same?

We keep the people, poses and composition. Restoration uses AI, so heavily damaged areas are rebuilt and very small details can differ slightly. 1 free revision is included if anything looks off.

Can you restore more than 2 photos?

Yes, place another order for each extra 2 photos, or email us for a bulk quote.

Is my photo private?

Your photos are used only to make your order and are never posted publicly.
