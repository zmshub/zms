FAQ

What photos should I upload?

1 to 3 clear photos of the product on its own, in daylight if possible, from the front and one angle. A normal phone photo is fine.

What do I get?

5 different AI product images, each in 1080×1080 (square) and 1080×1350 (portrait for Instagram), so 10 JPG files, by email within 24 hours.

Can I choose the style?

Yes. Tell us at checkout: studio, lifestyle, festive, minimal, luxury, or describe your own. If you leave it blank we pick styles that suit your product.

Will the product look exactly the same?

We keep your product's shape, colour and look. AI can simplify tiny details such as small printed label text, so for packaging with lots of fine print, send a sharp, close photo. 1 free revision is included.

Where can I use the images?

Anywhere you sell: Instagram, WhatsApp catalogue, Amazon, Meesho, Flipkart, Etsy, Shopify or your website.
