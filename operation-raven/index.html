Questions

How many players, and what ages?

1 to 8 players per squad, ages 8 to 14. Younger kids can play with a grown-up reader. For bigger parties, print one set per squad.

How long does it take?

Plan 45 to 60 minutes. Squads vary a lot, and the hint cards help anyone who gets stuck.

What do I need?

A printer (or a print shop), scissors, six envelopes, pencils and a timer. A small mirror helps in Room 4 but a bright window works too. No padlocks, apps or internet.

What are the puzzles?

A shift-code message you crack with a cut-out cipher wheel, a locker logic puzzle, picture sums with a gadget look-up, a mirror-writing riddle and a pigpen symbol code. Each answer is checked at a self-checking Door Lock.

Is there an answer key?

Yes. The Host Guide has a 10-minute setup, a script to read aloud, the answer key and a walk-through of every puzzle. Keep it away from the players.

What paper sizes are included?

US Letter and A4, each with a colour player pack and an ink-saver player pack on plain white, plus the Host Guide: 6 PDFs and a README.
