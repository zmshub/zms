Questions

Straight answers before you order.

How fast will I get my pack?

Within 24 hours of receiving both your completed intake form and your payment. If anything in your form is unclear, we'll ask once and the 24 hours start when we have the answer.

Do you post on my accounts?

No. We never log in to or post on your accounts. You publish the content yourself, whenever you like.

Do you guarantee more sales or higher rankings?

No. Good, consistent content helps, but results depend on your business, timing and audience, so we never promise sales, rankings or specific outcomes.

Who approves the offers and claims?

You do. Promo ideas are suggestions; run only the offers, prices and claims you approve and can legally provide.

Do you take the photos or videos?

No. Each post concept tells you exactly what to shoot on your phone, with on-screen text ideas.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Small businesses that serve customers: cafés, restaurants, salons, clinics, fitness studios, tutors, cleaners, repair shops, photographers, home services and local retail.

Which countries do you work with?

Businesses in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and other English-speaking markets. Everything is written in English for your local customers.

How do I pay?

Pay by card via a secure Razorpay link sent with your confirmation email. Prices are in USD.

What does the revision include?

One round of edits to the text in your pack. Send all your changes in one reply within 7 days of delivery.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Yes. There is no contract: tell us before your next month starts and we simply stop.
