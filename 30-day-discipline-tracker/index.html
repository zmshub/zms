Does it work in Google Sheets?

Yes. You get a ready-made Google Sheets copy with real tick boxes (File, Make a copy), plus .xlsx files for Microsoft Excel, LibreOffice and Apple Numbers.

How many habits can I track?

Up to 8 daily habits or non-negotiables. For a first challenge we suggest 3 to 5.

How are streaks counted?

Current streak is the number of days in a row you have done a habit up to today; today counts once you tick it, so an unticked today does not break your streak. Longest streak is your best run in the 30 days.

Can I print it?

Yes. The printable PDF comes in A4 and US Letter: commitment page, habit setup, 30-day tick grid, habit chains, 4 weekly reviews, a final review, a mood/energy/sleep graph and a completion certificate.
