Questions

How many players do I need?

Six to eight guests plus a host who can also play. Eight characters are included. With 6 or 7 players, drop Eddie Malone and then Violet Ashby; the host reads their clue cards from page 6 of the Host Guide so no clue is lost.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Print the kit, cut out a few cards and set out snacks. The Host Guide has the opening speech and a script for each of the three rounds. No acting skills are needed.

How long does it take?

About 90 to 120 minutes: three rounds (alibis, clues, reveal), then a vote and the confession.

Can the host play too?

Yes. The murderer is built into the character booklets, so a host who does not open the SOLUTION file can play a character and still be surprised.

Is it solvable by logic?

Yes. Every clue is on paper, the murderer is the only one who may lie, and the evidence cards cannot be argued with. A set of 9 hint cards in 3 levels helps if the table gets stuck.

Is it suitable for kids?

The recommended age is 14 and up. It is a classic whodunit with no graphic scenes. Drinks are only period detail; the party menu is alcohol-free.

What paper sizes are included?

Both US Letter and A4, plus ink-saver versions on plain white paper. Print at Actual size (100%), not Fit to page.
