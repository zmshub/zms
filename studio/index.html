FAQ

Do you offer marketing, SEO or ads management?

No. We are a creative, AI and tech studio: we make the visuals, brands, websites and AI systems. We don't run social media accounts, SEO or ad campaigns.

Do you edit videos?

No video editing of your footage. Our AI Videos, Reels & Shorts are fully AI-generated new footage.

How fast is delivery?

Most AI image, headshot and logo packages arrive in 24–72 hours. Websites take 5–21 days and apps 1–4 weeks. The clock starts once payment and your brief are in.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes, we work remotely with brands, agencies and creators worldwide. Prices are shown in INR for India and USD for international clients.

How do I pay?

Send your brief and we reply with a quote and a secure payment link. Fixed packages are paid upfront; custom projects of ₹10,000 / $150 or more are 50% to start and 50% before handoff. We don't do refund & return ✌️

Who owns the work?

Once paid, you can use the final deliverables for your business. AI-generated images can't always be registered as trademarks, so for a trademark we recommend our Pro or Premium logo with vector files and your own legal check.

What extra costs could there be?

Only third-party fees you choose: domain and hosting, AI/API usage for chatbots, or automation tool subscriptions. We tell you upfront.

Can I get something not listed?

Yes. Tell us what you need and we will quote it within 24 hours.
