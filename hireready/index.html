Why ATS-friendly matters

Most resumes are read by software first.

Job portals, big companies and campus drives use an Applicant Tracking System to scan and rank resumes. Fancy two-column designs with icons and skill bars often come out as jumbled text. These templates are built to stay readable.

  • One column, no tables

    No text boxes, graphics or photos hiding your details from the scanner.

  • Standard headings

    Education, Skills, Projects, Experience — the words an ATS looks for.

  • Safe fonts

    Arial, Calibri, Georgia and Times New Roman at readable sizes.

  • Fresher-first sections

    Objective, CGPA / percentage, internships, projects, NPTEL & certifications.
