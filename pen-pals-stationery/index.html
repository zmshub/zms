Questions

What paper sizes are included?

Everything comes in both US Letter and A4, in separate folders. Print at Actual size (100%), not Fit to page.

Is this a Canva or Word template?

No. It is a ready-to-print PDF set, so there is nothing to edit or install. Just open, print and write by hand. The blank versions are there if you want to add your own text.

Can I use the stamps to mail a letter?

The stamps are decorative craft graphics for envelopes, journals and play. They are not real postage, so use real stamps when you send a letter.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. There is a wide-ruled version of every design with a dotted guide line, a fill-in first letter, an About Me page and 40 question cards. The start guide also covers pen pal safety tips for young writers.

What paper should I print on?

Plain paper works well. 100 to 120 gsm (28 to 32 lb) feels nicer for letters, and card stock is best for the envelopes.

Can I use it for a class or a club?

Yes, for personal, family and classroom use, including pen pal programmes you run. Please do not resell or share the files.
