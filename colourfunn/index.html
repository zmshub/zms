Good to know

Quick answers for parents and teachers.

What do I get?

PDF files in A4 and US Letter: 20 pages per book, plus a “This book belongs to” page.

Best paper?

Thick paper, printed on one side only.

Can I use it in class?

Yes, for personal and classroom use. Please don’t resell or share the PDF file.

Payment didn’t work?

Tap “Payment not going through? Try another way” for another payment route, or email us.

Are these printable coloring pages for kids available in India?

Yes. Colourfunn coloring pages are made for families and schools in India: pay ₹99 per book (or ₹249 for all 3) with any UPI app and get the PDF download link by email.

What age are these coloring pages for?

Kids aged 3–8. The bold outlines and big shapes suit toddlers, preschool, LKG/UKG and early primary classes.

Can I print the coloring PDF at home?

Yes. Each book is a printable PDF in A4 and US Letter sizes, so you can print the pages at home or at a print shop as many times as you need.
